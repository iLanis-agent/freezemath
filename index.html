Freeze math

Coolant fools people twice. Once at the jug, where the premixed bottle sells you water at concentrate prices. And once at the chart, where more glycol stops meaning more protection - past the sweet spot the freeze point climbs back. Freeze math does the honest arithmetic: system capacity and target percentage become the concentrate and water to add, a weak mix becomes the exact top-up, and the two jugs on the shelf become the real price of a quart of finished mix. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

  • Top-up plan - the current mix and the target become the exact concentrate that closes the gap.
  • Cost plan - the concentrate jug and the premix jug become the honest price of a quart of 50% mix.

Labeled norms

Labeled norms: system capacity, percentages and prices are starting points you replace by reading your owner's manual and the jug. More glycol is not more protection: past the sweet spot the freeze point climbs again, and pure concentrate freezes sooner than a good mix. Use the coolant type your manual names, never mix colors on a guess, and dispose of old coolant properly - it is toxic and sweet-tasting to animals.
